Car Travel Tips!

Whether you're taking a road trip, or simply a quick drive to the vet, animals have various needs when traveling by car. People travel with their pets in this way all the time, so here are some tips to make your pets car ride as safe and comfortable as possible! Many of these tips and products can also be used in other methods of transportation, so they aren't just limited to the car. With that being said, lets jump into some tips!

  • Get your pets used to the car and carrier
  • Protect your car seats
  • Never leave your pets unattended!
  • Clean up after your pets and stop for breaks
  • Try to keep trips short

Plane Travel Tips!

Air travel can be stressful for animals, and every airline has its own rules and requirements for traveling with pets. From choosing the right carrier to helping your pet feel comfortable, here are some tips to make your pet's flight as safe and comfortable as possible! With that being said, let's get ready for takeoff!

Check the Airline's Regulations

Before booking your flight, make sure to check the airline's regulations for traveling with pets. Airlines can have different rules regarding pet carrier dimensions, weight limits, fees, breed restrictions, and required health documents. Some destinations may also require vaccinations, health certificates, or other paperwork before your pet can enter. Contact the airline ahead of time to confirm that your pet can travel on your chosen flight and that you understand all the requirements. Planning ahead can help prevent unexpected problems at the airport and make your journey much smoother.

Choose an Airline-Approved Carrier

Choosing the right carrier is essential when flying with your pet. Your carrier must meet the airline's specific requirements, including its size, ventilation, and security features. For pets traveling in the cabin, the carrier will usually need to fit underneath the seat in front of you. Make sure your pet has enough room to stand, turn around, and lie down comfortably. A carrier that is secure and appropriately sized can help keep your pet safe throughout the journey. Products such as SHERPA pet carriers offer options designed for air travel, but always confirm that your chosen model meets your airline's requirements before purchasing it.

Help Your Pet Feel Comfortable

Airports and airplanes can be unfamiliar and overwhelming environments for pets. Before your trip, give your pet time to become comfortable with their carrier by leaving it open at home and encouraging them to explore it with treats, toys, or familiar bedding. Gradually introducing your pet to the carrier can help reduce stress during travel. For cats, products such as Feliway Classic Spray may help with some stress-related behaviors. Follow the product instructions and allow sprayed surfaces to dry before your cat comes into contact with them. Remember that every pet reacts differently to travel, so preparing them in advance is important.

Consider Pet Insurance and a Veterinary Checkup

Before traveling by plane, consider scheduling a visit with your veterinarian to discuss whether your pet is healthy enough to fly. This is especially important for pets with existing health conditions, very young or old animals, and short-nosed breeds that may have difficulty breathing. Your veterinarian can also advise you about motion sickness, anxiety, and any travel documents your pet may need. Pet insurance is another option to consider when preparing for a trip. Depending on the policy, it may help cover eligible unexpected veterinary expenses. Check your policy carefully for exclusions, geographic restrictions, and coverage limitations before traveling.

Know Your Pet's Travel Arrangements

Depending on the airline, your pet may be permitted to travel in the cabin with you, or they may need to travel through a separate checked-pet or cargo service. Not every airline offers every option, and restrictions can depend on your pet's size, breed, carrier, and destination. If your pet is traveling in the cabin, confirm the carrier dimensions and under-seat requirements. If your pet must travel separately, carefully review the airline's procedures and discuss any concerns with your veterinarian. Never assume that your pet can travel on your flight without making the necessary arrangements in advance. Knowing exactly how your pet will travel can help you prepare for a safer, less stressful journey.

Final Reminder

A safe flight starts long before you arrive at the airport. Check your airline's requirements, choose an appropriate carrier, help your pet become comfortable with it, and speak with your veterinarian before departure. Make sure you have all the necessary documents and have confirmed your pet's travel arrangements. With a little preparation, you can help make air travel a more comfortable experience for both you and your furry companion. Safe travels and happy tails!
